How to play

Swap tiles until each row and column holds exactly the atoms of the compound named beside it.

🎈Hydrogen🫁Oxygen🍽️Stomach acid 💧WaterHOCa 🔥QuicklimeOH
→
🎈Hydrogen🫁Oxygen🍽️Stomach acid 💧WaterHOH 🔥QuicklimeOCa

Water is H₂O and quicklime is CaO. Swapping the Ca down for the H makes both at once: a combo.

  1. Each row has a compound on the left and each column one on top, by its common name. The formula is up to you. Tap a name for a clue: how many atoms it has and a fact about it.
  2. Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it (left, right, up or down) to swap them. You can also swipe a tile toward its neighbour.
  3. A line is made when its atoms match the formula exactly, in any order. Blank tiles fill the leftover cells, so a 3-atom compound on a 5×5 board needs two blanks in its line. You score, the line fills with new tiles and it gets a new compound.
  4. A compound is worth 5 points per atom: water (H₂O) is 15, rust (Fe₂O₃) is 25. Make more than one with a single swap for a combo: 2 compounds score ×2, 3 score ×3, and so on. New tiles can set off a chain reaction, which adds to the combo.
  5. Every swap costs one from your supply. The round ends when you run out.
  6. Hint spends 2 swaps to plan a full route to a compound. The tiles to swap glow one pair at a time, and the target is outlined. Follow the plan to the end at no extra cost; any other swap cancels it. Shuffle deals a new board, free, 3 times per round.

Every compound can be made from the board it was dealt with. Bigger boards ask for bigger molecules. Once you've made a compound, its formula goes in your lab notebook and shows when you tap its name. The Daily board is the same for everyone, and your first finish is the one that counts.
